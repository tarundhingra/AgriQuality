AGRI-COMMODITY QUALITY GUIDELINES (DEMONSTRATION)

WHEAT:
- Moisture: Ideal range is 10.0% to 12.0%. Anything above 12.0% risks fungal growth and requires a RETEST or REJECT depending on severity. Moisture above 14.0% is highly dangerous for storage.
- Protein: Ideal range is 11.0% to 14.0%. Below 11.0% is considered low-grade for baking.
- Broken Grains: Must be below 5.0%. 
- Foreign Matter: Must be strictly below 1.0%.

RICE:
- Moisture: Ideal is below 14.0%.
- Broken Grains: Premium grade allows up to 5.0%. Standard grade allows up to 15.0%.
- Foreign Matter: Must be below 0.5%.

MAIZE:
- Moisture: Ideal is 13.0% to 14.0%. Above 15.0% risks aflatoxin.
- Broken Grains: Must be below 4.0%.
- Foreign Matter: Must be below 1.5%.

PROCUREMENT ACTION RULES:
- If any critical parameter (Moisture or Foreign Matter) is slightly out of bounds, mandate a RETEST.
- If multiple parameters are out of bounds, or one is severely out of bounds (e.g., Wheat moisture > 14%), REJECT the lot.
- Check supplier history: If a supplier has a deteriorating trend, downgrade borderline ACCEPTs to RETESTs.
